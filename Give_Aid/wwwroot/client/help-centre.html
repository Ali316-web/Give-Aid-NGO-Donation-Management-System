Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a donation?
Go to the Donate page, choose an amount and cause, then complete the payment form. You'll receive a confirmation with a reference number immediately.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
GIVE-AID issues donation receipts for all completed transactions. Please check with your local tax authority regarding deductibility.
How do I register as a member?
Click Register in the top navigation, fill in your details and accept the terms & conditions. You'll be able to log in immediately after.
How can I join a programme?
Open the Programmes page, select a programme, and click "I'm Interested" on the details page. The organizing NGO will contact you with next steps.
How do I raise a query or complaint?
Log in and go to Raise a Query from your dashboard. Choose a category, describe your issue, and our support team will reply within 48 hours.

Still need help?

Our support team typically replies within 48 hours.
