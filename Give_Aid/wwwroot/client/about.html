Our story

About GIVE-AID

GIVE-AID is a network connecting donors with a community of associated NGOs — pooling resources, transparency and reach so help arrives faster and goes further.

What We Do Our Mission Our Team Careers Achievements Supporters
What we do

Connecting resources to where they matter most

We vet and onboard associated NGOs, run transparent donation pipelines by cause, and organize joint programmes across healthcare, education and welfare — so donors can trust exactly where their contribution lands.

Our mission

Shelter, dignity and opportunity for every community we reach

We believe lasting change grows from local roots. Our mission is to strengthen grassroots NGOs with funding, visibility and shared infrastructure — not to replace them.
